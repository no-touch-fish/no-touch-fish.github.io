Research Interests
I am currently interested in LLM agents, LLM post-training, trustworthy LLMs, and LLMs for engineering applications. My recent work spans agent design, post-training, safety, reasoning, and engineering-oriented adaptation for large language models.
  • LLM agents for multi-turn planning, reasoning, and strategic interaction.
  • LLM post-training and alignment, including supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, reward design, and domain-specific adaptation.
  • Trustworthy and engineering-oriented LLMs, including safety, hallucination mitigation, and applications to various engineering tasks.
