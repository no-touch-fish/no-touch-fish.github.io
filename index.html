Research Interests
My research focuses on how LLMs and autonomous agents can learn and improve from feedback and experience. I am particularly interested in:
  • Post-training methods for improving reasoning and decision-making.
  • Recursive self-improvement (RSI) that enables agents to learn from multi-step interactions and experimental outcomes.
  • Autonomous agents that can advance scientific discovery and solve complex engineering problems.
